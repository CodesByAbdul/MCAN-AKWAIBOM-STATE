Who We Are

What is MCAN?

The Muslim Corpers' Association of Nigeria (MCAN) is the official body uniting all Muslim NYSC corps members across every state in Nigeria. We provide spiritual, social, and professional support throughout your service year — starting from Day 1 at camp.

In Akwa Ibom State, MCAN is active across the orientation camp and throughout the state. From the moment you register, we connect you with your fellow Muslim corpers, ensure you know where Salah is held, and keep you informed of every activity that matters.

We organise congregational prayers, Islamic lectures, da'wah activities, social welfare programs, skill acquisition workshops, and community bonding events — keeping your iman strong through camp and beyond.
